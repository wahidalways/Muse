# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

People who want to find and play music files saved on their Android phone.

## Product Purpose

Muse helps listeners browse, organize, and play music stored on their device. Success means getting from an existing local music file to playback quickly and keeping playback controls easy to reach.

## Positioning

Muse is an offline-first local music player. It does not require an account or a streaming service, and the listener chooses which device folders are included.

## Operating Context

Listeners use Muse on an Android phone to scan local music, choose included folders, search and sort tracks, and control playback while browsing or listening.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app is built with Expo and React Native.
- The library reads music stored on the device.
- Existing folder include/block controls, search, and sort behavior must remain available.
- Playback includes a mini-player, full player, seeking, previous and next track controls, shuffle, repeat, sleep timer, and an expandable Up Next queue.
- Listening history and Home listening statistics are stored locally.
- Themes and accent colors are user-selectable.
- Use familiar Android navigation, safe areas, and touch behavior.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Muse.
- The user provided a Spotify-inspired `DESIGN.md` as a source of general music-player design cues. Adapt ideas to Muse; do not copy Spotify branding or distinctive visual details.

## Evidence on Hand

- The existing Expo app and its local library and playback implementation are the source of truth for supported behavior.
- No external streaming catalog, account system, testimonials, or performance claims are part of the product.

## Product Principles

- Help listeners find and start their own music quickly.
- Keep listening control available while browsing.
- Keep local files and listening history on the device.
- Preserve user control over included folders and playback behavior.
